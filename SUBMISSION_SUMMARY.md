---

### 2. The Problem Statement (Addressed directly from prompt)
Small and marginal farmers across emerging economies lack access to data-driven agricultural guidance. Relying on traditional methods without satellite data, soil health analytics, and climate forecasting leads to crop failure and threatens food security. Furthermore, the absence of shared digital infrastructure blocks cross-border collaboration on climate-resilient farming.

---

### 3. Proposed Solution & Key Innovations
Inspired by the **BRICS AgriN initiative**, we engineered an open-source, scalable **Digital Public Good (DPG)** that unifies:
1. **Hyper-Local Agro-Advisories via AI**:
   - Integrates microclimate telemetry (temperature, humidity, evapotranspiration, solar radiation) with a 7-day regenerative farm action planner.
   - Multilingual AI Agro-Ecological Copilot equipped with voice readout for rural smallholder accessibility.
2. **Sentinel-2 Satellite Earth Observation & Soil Health Hub**:
   - 10m-resolution multispectral NDVI vegetative vigor analyzer.
   - Interactive Soil Health Card that computes a real-time **Regenerative Soil Index (0–100)** assessing Soil Organic Carbon (SOC), water retention, and microbial activity.
   - 1-click regenerative bio-restoration simulation.
3. **Regenerative Crop Recommender & Carbon Credit Model**:
   - 3-tier polyculture engine (Cash Crop + Nitrogen-Fixing Companion Legume + Biomass Cover Crop).
   - Zero-budget biological inputs pharmacopeia (Jeevamrutha, Bokashi, Biochar matrix).
   - Smallholder Carbon Revenue & ROI Calculator for voluntary carbon markets.
4. **Computer Vision Crop Disease & Pest Clinic**:
   - Multimodal diagnostic engine with scanning animation and confidence rating.
   - 6 instant judge-ready test samples (Tomato Early Blight, Rice Blast, Maize Fall Armyworm, Wheat Yellow Rust, Coffee Leaf Rust, Potato Late Blight) + live photo upload.
   - Zero-chemical organic and biological prescriptions (Trichoderma, Bacillus subtilis, Neem oil, Push-Pull companion planting).
5. **BRICS Digital Public Good & Multilateral Interoperability Mesh**:
   - **Transboundary Threat Surveillance Radar**: Synchronized alerts for migratory pests (Fall Armyworm) and climate shocks (El Niño dry spells) shared across ICAR (India), EMBRAPA (Brazil), ARC (South Africa), CAAS (China), and RAS (Russia).
   - **Open-Agri REST API Console**: Interactive test suite with downloadable OpenAPI 3.0 specification.
   - **Federated Model Registry**: Privacy-preserving federated AI models that collaborate without centralizing private land records.
   - Multilingual support for English, Hindi, Portuguese, Mandarin, and Russian.

---

### 4. How BRICS Cooperation is Realized
- **Shared Data Models**: Standardized OpenAPI endpoints conforming to FAO AGROVOC and GeoJSON standards.
- **Multilateral Institutional Alignment**: Connects the agricultural research networks of India, Brazil, South Africa, China, and Russia.
- **Digital Public Good Compliance**: Adheres to the 9 criteria of the Digital Public Goods Alliance (DPGA Standard v1.4).
